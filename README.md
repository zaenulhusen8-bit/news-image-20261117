A make.fun entry for the **news-image-20261117** competition: one image that sums up
the world news of Tuesday 17 November 2026.

## The day in five stories

- **Lead — Kerch Bridge collapses after a Ukrainian strike.** A road span falls into
  the strait; Russian missiles hit Kyiv's bridges in reply.
- **Asia — Trump lands in Manila**, on his way to Shenzhen for talks with Xi and Putin
  on nuclear risks and Ukraine.
- **The Gulf — Tankers hit by mines in Hormuz**; a third US carrier arrives in the
  Middle East.
- **Space — Vulcan rocket fails**, destroying 40 Amazon internet satellites minutes
  after launch from Cape Canaveral.
- **Washington — Pentagon fails its audit again**, a ninth straight year without a
  clean opinion on $4.8 trillion in assets.

## How it is built

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` byte-for-byte to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, so both runs write identical bytes. The image is a
1024×1024 PNG under 1 MiB: a generated hero photo of the collapsed bridge, composited
with crisp headline text.

Sources: BBC, AP, Reuters, Al Jazeera.
